# FlowKit — launch submissions

Live product: https://hwhsinyu-del.github.io/velora-landing/

**Product name:** FlowKit

**Category:** Productivity / CRM / Quotes / Small business

**Tagline:** Free quotes and follow-ups for small businesses.

**Short description:** FlowKit helps freelancers and small service businesses create quotes, track client status, and remember the next follow-up. No account is required. Data stays in the browser and can be exported as CSV or JSON.

**Long description:** Lost a client after sending a quote? FlowKit offers a lightweight way to organize estimates and follow-up dates without signing up for a full CRM. Create a quote, record whether a prospect is waiting, in discussion, won or lost, and export your records. The free browser-based MVP works in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Spanish. Current limitations: no cloud sync, no push notifications, no checkout. We are inviting early users to tell us which features matter most.

**Price:** Free beta; no paid plan available yet.

**Audience:** Freelancers, design studios, cleaning/repair services, small B2B suppliers.

**Keywords:** quote maker, follow up tracker, free CRM, small business, estimates, sales pipeline.
